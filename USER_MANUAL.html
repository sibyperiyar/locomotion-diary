0. Introduction: Why this App?

Locomotion Diary is a dual-purpose tool designed to master two distinct but critical aspects of your life: Retrospective Memory (your past travels) and Prospective Security (your financial legacy).

📖 Part 1: My Diary (The Mirror of Your Life)

In a digital age, our movements are tracked by tech giants, but we rarely get to see or own that story ourselves. Locomotion Diary reclaims this data.

  • Visualizing the Invisible: It takes the raw, messy GPS coordinates collected by Google and transforms them into a beautiful, readable narrative of your life's journey.
  • Manual Journaling (No Google Needed): You don't need "Big Tech" data to use this. You can start a fresh diary from scratch, manually adding your life's important events, visits, and memories day-by-day.
  • Mindful reflection: By seeing where you spent your time—how many hours in traffic, how many walks in the park—you gain a powerful mirror to reflect on your lifestyle choices.
  • Private Memory Keeping: Unlike social media, this diary is private. It helps you preserve the small details—the cafe you visited on a rainy Tuesday, the road trip route you took—that would otherwise be forgotten.

📔 Part 2: Legacy Binder (The Safety Net)

1. Managing Digital Complexity
With our lives spread across various apps, portals, and paperless billing, our financial footprint has become invisible. A legacy binder consolidates these scattered digital threads (passwords, customer IDs, and hidden investments) into a single point of reference.

2. Providing a Safety Net for Your Family
In the event of an emergency, family members are often overwhelmed. Forcing them to navigate a bureaucratic maze is an added burden. This binder acts as a "manual" for your life, allowing loved ones to manage affairs with dignity and clarity.

3. Financial Discipline & "Net Worth" Clarity
Maintaining this planner forces a regular audit of your life. It highlights dormant accounts, upcoming renewals, and your true liabilities, helping you make informed decisions about retirement and estate planning.

4. Protection Against "Information Blackouts"
Technology is fragile. Phones break, accounts get locked. Having a structured record (which you can export/print) ensures your most vital information remains accessible even during technical failures.

5. Seamless Transition of Wealth
Billions of rupees lie in unclaimed accounts because nominees were unaware they existed. By documenting every deed and fund here, we ensure your legacy is successfully transferred to the next generation.

3.5 Startup Guide 🚀

Welcome! Follow these steps to set up your secure diary.

Step 1: Security First (The Master Key)

When you first open the app, you will be asked to create a Master Password.

  • Why? This password encrypts EVERYTHING (Diary & Binder).
  • Constraint: We cannot recover this for you.
  • Action: Create a strong password and immediately go to Binder > Security to download your 🚑 Emergency Kit.

Step 1.5: Enable Biometrics (Optional) 👆

If you have a supported device (Windows Hello / TouchID):

  • After setting your password, go to Binder > Security.
  • Click Enroll Biometrics.
  • This allows you to unlock the app with a simple fingerprint or face scan next time!
  • Note: Requires HTTPS (secure hosting) or Localhost.

Step 2: Choosing Your Path

How do you want to use Locomotion Diary?

🅰️ The Google Migrator (Import History)

If you have years of Google Maps Timeline history:

  1. Complete Step 1 (Set Password).
  2. Follow the JSON Import Instructions (Section 7).
  3. Drag & drop your Google file. The app will encrypt and suck in all your past travels.

🅱️ The Fresh Journalist (Start New)

If you want a private manual journal:

  1. Complete Step 1 (Set Password).
  2. Click Current Month to start writing today.
  3. Use the Date Picker to go back in time and add memories manually.

4. Proximity Radius Setup

Located in Places > Settings.

  • Definition: The distance within which two GPS points are considered the "Same Place".
  • Small (50m): High precision. Distinguishes neighbors.
  • Large (500m): Groups broad areas (e.g., "Airport Complex") into one location.

7. Importing JSON Data

For Google Timeline Users:
  1. Go to Google Takeout.
  2. Deselect All first.
  3. Scroll down and check Location History (Timeline).
  4. Click on "Multiple Formats" check that it says JSON (not KML).
  5. Click Next Step > Create Export.
  6. Download the ZIP file and extract it.
  7. Look for a file named Records.json (or individual year files like 2024_JANUARY.json).

Once you have the file:

  1. Open Locomotion Diary.
  2. Drag & Drop the Records.json file onto the center of the screen (or click the folder icon to select it).
  3. Wait for the import to finish (it stays offline).

8. Manual Creation (Non-Google Users)

No Google Data? No problem! You can use this app as a pure travel journal from scratch.

How to Start Fresh:
  1. Open the app. Even if the "Drop Zone" is visible, just look for the ➕ Add button in the navigation (or load an empty year via the dropdown if available).
  2. Create a Day: Add your first entry. The system will create the "Day" container for you.

Event Types:

  • 📍 Stationary: Log a visit to a place (Hotel, Restaurant, Park). You can use the map to pin the exact spot.
  • 🚀 Moving: Log a journey. Provide a Start and End point to draw a line on the map.

Tip: Use the "Places" manager to save your favorite spots (Home, Office) for quick auto-complete later.

When you export as JSON, the app must DECRYPT your data to make it readable (for you or other apps).

12. Naming Locations & Place Management

You can rename generic coordinates (e.g., "51.50, -0.12") to meaningful names like "Home" or "Work".

Renaming a Place

  1. Find an entry for the location you want to name.
  2. Click the Pencil Icon (✎) or "Edit" button next to the location name.
  3. Option A: Type the new name (e.g., "My Office").
  4. Option B (New): Click "📍 Auto-Detect Name (OSM)" to let the app find the real address map for you!
  5. Click Save.
  6. Effect: This renames all future and past visits to this spot (within the Proximity Radius).

Managing & Backing Up Places

Your saved places are stored in your browser. To keep them safe or move them to another device:

  1. Click the **Location Manager** button (📍 Places) in the main menu.
  2. **backup:** Click **📤 Export Places** to download a `locomotion_places_backup.json` file to your computer.
  3. **Restore:** Click **📥 Import Places** and select your backup file to restore your list of named places.
  4. Tip: Do this before clearing your browser history!

20. Legacy Binder & Financial Planner 📔

What is it? A private, secure place to organize your life's critical documents (Insurance, Property, IDs) alongside your diary.

Key Features

  • 👪 Multi-Member: Manage records for yourself, spouse, parents, or children.
  • 📂 Recursive Folders: Create unlimited sub-folders (e.g., Health > Insurance > Policy).
  • 🔔 Expiry Alerts: Automatically tracks dates (like Passport Expiry) and warns you 60 days in advance.
  • 🔒 Password Masking: Store digital legacy pins securely (masked as ••••••).
  • 🇮🇳 Global Master List: Comes with a pre-loaded template for Indian Civil & Financial documents.
⚠️ Important: Your data lives in your Browser's Memory. If you clear your history/cache, it will be DELETED. Please click "📤 Backup" regularly to save a JSON file to your computer.

How to Use

  1. Access: Click the "📔 Binder" button in the top navigation.
  2. Add Member: Click "➕ 👤" to create a profile. It will auto-load the default master list.
  3. Edit Structure:
    • Click "+ Sub-Section" to add nested folders.
    • Click "+ Add Field" to add data points (Text, Date, Password).
  4. Search: Click "🔍 Search" to find any policy number or document across ALL family members instantly.

Automatic Upcoming Alerts 🔔

The app automatically scans your binder for urgent dates.

How to trigger an alert?
The system looks for fields that meet two conditions:
  1. Type: The field must be a Date field.
  2. Name: The title must contain one of these keywords:
    • "Expiry" (e.g., Passport Expiry)
    • "Renew" (e.g., Renewal Date)
    • "Due" (e.g., Payment Due)
Matches within the next 60 days will appear in the dashboard.

Visual Template Editing (Advanced) 🛠️

Want to change the default list for everyone?

  1. Design your perfect folder structure in the app using the buttons.
  2. Click "💾 Tpl" (Save Template).
  3. This downloads a file named binder_template.js.
  4. Replace the file in your app folder with this new one.
  5. Now, every new member you add will start with YOUR custom structure!

21. Security, Encryption & Recovery 🔐

Zero-Knowledge Architecture:

Your Diary Events and Legacy Binder are both encrypted with AES-GCM-256 using your Master Password.

  • We (the developers) cannot see your data.
  • Your password is never sent to any server.
  • CRITICAL: If you lose your password, your data is lost forever. There is no "Forgot Password" link.

App Lock Screen

You can lock the entire application to prevent unauthorized access on a shared device.

  • Auto-Lock: The app locks automatically when you close the tab.
  • Manual Lock: Click the 🔒 Lock App button in the Binder Security sidebar.

👆 Biometric Unlock (Windows Hello / TouchID)

Enjoy password-free access while keeping your data encrypted.

  • How it works: We use your device's security chip (TPM) to safely store your encryption key.
  • Setup: Go to the Security Sidebar and click "Enroll Biometrics".
  • Requirements:
    • A device with Windows Hello (Face/Fingerprint) or TouchID.
    • The app must be served over a Secure Connection (HTTPS) or localhost.
    • It will NOT work if you just open the index.html file directly (file://).

🚑 Emergency Access Kit & Recovery

Because there is no email reset, you MUST handle your own "Plan B".

1. Creating a Backup (The "Golden Key")

  1. Go to Binder > Security sidebar section.
  2. Click 🚑 Emergency Kit.
  3. This downloads a special HTML file (e.g., Locomotion-Emergency-Kit.html).
  4. Includes: Your Master Password (Visible) + Full Encrypted Binder Backup.
  5. Store Safely: Print this out or save it to a physical USB drive. Do NOT email it to yourself unencrypted.

2. How to Restore Data

If you get locked out or switch devices, use the Unified Restore tool on the Lock Screen.

  • Click "♻️ Restore Backup".
  • Upload Binder HTML: Restores your Binder data AND your Master Password (auto-unlocks).
  • Upload Diary JSON: Restores your Diary events (requires you to be Unlocked first, or use Reset below).

3. Factory Reset (I Forgot My Password!)

If you completely lose your password and have no Emergency Kit, your data is inaccessible. To make the app usable again:

  1. Click "🗑️ Reset App" on the Lock Screen.
  2. Accept the warnings to DELETE ALL DATA.
  3. This wipes the encrypted database so you can start fresh.
  4. Recovery Tip: If you have a plain JSON backup of your diary, you can import it after resetting to recover your events.

22. Troubleshooting 🔧

If the app ever feels "stuck", looks weird, or doesn't update:

  1. Go to Settings ⚙️.
  2. Click 🔄 Force Reload App.
What this does: It performs a "Factory Reset" on the app's code (not your data). It clears old cached files and updates the interface to the latest version. Your diary entries are safe!
