# User Guide 📖

This guide will walk you through the core features of Locomotion Diary.

## Getting Started

### 1. Setting Up (First Run)

When you first open the app, you'll be asked to create a **Master Password**. This password encrypts your entire database. Make sure it's strong and memorable!

### 2. Choose Your Path

* **Google Migrator:** Import your existing Google Timeline history (JSON) to visualize years of travel.
* **Fresh Journalist:** Start a manual diary from scratch. Use the **➕ Add** button to create new days and events.

## Core Workflows

### 🗺️ Places & Locations

* **Renaming:** Click the pencil icon next to any coordinate (e.g., "51.50, -0.12") to give it a name like "Home" or "Starbucks".
* **Auto-Detect:** Use the **📍 Auto-Detect Name (OSM)** button to let OpenStreetMap find the address for you.
* **Proximity Radius:** Adjust in Settings (50m - 500m) to group nearby points together.

### ☁️ Enhancing Data

* **Weather:** Click the **☁️ Weather** button to fetch historical weather data for your trips.
* **Photos:** Click **🖼️ Photos** to see localized photo links from your Google Photos library.

### 📔 Legacy Binder

A secure vault for your critical documents.

* **Add Member:** Manage records for your whole family.
* **Expiry Alerts:** The app auto-scans for dates like "Passport Expiry" and warns you 60 days in advance.
* **Templates:** Customize the folder structure for all new members using the Template Editor.

## Exporting Your Data

### PDF E-Book 🖨️

Turn your diary into a beautiful printable book.

1. Filter to your desired date range.
2. Click **🖨️ PDF**.
3. *Tip:* Enable "Background Graphics" in your printer settings for full color.

### JSON Backup 💾

Regularly backup your data!

1. Click **💾 JSON**.
2. Save the file to a secure location.
3. **Note:** This file is decrypted plain text, so keep it safe!
