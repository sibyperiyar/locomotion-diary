## 📖 What is this?

Locomotion Diary serves two main purposes:

1. **Retrospective Memory (Travel Diary):**
    * Visualize your Google Timeline history on beautiful maps.
    * Manually log your journeys without Big Tech tracking.
    * Create printable PDF E-Books of your life's travels.

2. **Prospective Security (Legacy Binder):**
    * A secure, encrypted vault for your critical documents (Insurance, IDs, Passwords).
    * Ensures your family has access to vital info in case of emergencies.
    * Run entirely offline in your browser—**Zero Knowledge** architecture.

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Offline-First:** Works without an internet connection (PWA).
* **Encrypted:** Uses AES-GCM-256 encryption for your Binder.
* **Biometric Unlock:** Support for Windows Hello / TouchID.
* **Smart Automation:** Fetches historical weather and detects place names automatically.
