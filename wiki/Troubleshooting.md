# Troubleshooting 🔧

Having issues? Here are some common fixes.

## The App is "Stuck" or Not Updating

If the interface looks broken or you suspect you're on an old version:

1. Go to **Settings ⚙️**.
2. Click **🔄 Force Reload App**.
3. This clears the app's cache and reloads the latest code. **Your data is safe!**

## Forgot Password?

If you lost your Master Password and didn't create an Emergency Kit:

1. Click **🗑️ Reset App** on the Lock Screen.
2. This behaves like a "Factory Reset" and **deletes all data**.
3. You can then start fresh or import a previous JSON backup.

## Map Not Loading?

* Check your internet connection. Map tiles require internet access.
* Ensure you are not blocking OpenStreetMap domains.

## Photos Not Showing?

The **🖼️ Photos** feature generates *links* to your Google Photos search. It does not embed the photos directly into the app database (to save space and privacy).

* Ensure you are logged into Google Photos in another tab.
* Check that the date on your diary entry matches your photo dates.
