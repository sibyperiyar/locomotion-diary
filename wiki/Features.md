# Features Deep Dive 🌟

## 🗺️ Interactive Maps

Visualize your life on a map.

* **Daily Route:** See the path you took, including stops and travel modes.
* **Heatmaps:** (Coming Soon) Visualize your most visited areas.
* **Privacy:** Maps are rendered using OpenStreetMap tiles. No capabilities are tracking you.

## ☁️ Weather & Context

* **Historical Data:** The app connects to Open-Meteo to fetch the weather for the *exact time and place* you were there.
* **No API Key Needed:** It works out of the box!

## 📔 The Legacy Binder

A completely separate, encrypted vault within the app.

* **Purpose:** Store "Life Admin" documents—Insurance, Investments, IDs.
* **Heir-Ready:** Designed so your family can handle your affairs if you are incapacitated.
* **Templates:** Comes with a pre-built template for Indian Civil & Financial documents, but fully customizable.

## 🤖 AI Summaries

* **Local AI:** Generates a text summary of your day ("You spent 3 hours driving...") using simple logic on your device.
* **Privacy:** No data is sent to OpenAI or Gemini. It's all standard code running locally!
